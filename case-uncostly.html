Purchase and rental in one catalogue

Uncostly sells and rents security and surveillance equipment. Buyers compare specs across several products before deciding, and some only want to rent.

The Challenge

The catalogue had to span 12+ categories and support both outright purchase and equipment rental, with tools that help technical buyers compare specs side by side.

What I Built

  • WooCommerce catalogue structure across 12+ product categories.
  • Side-by-side comparison tables for spec-heavy purchase decisions.
  • Wishlist and order-tracking functionality.
  • A dedicated Rent section, separate from the standard purchase flow.

Key Improvements

Security equipment buyers usually compare specs across several products before committing, and part of the demand is short-term rental. Building both flows into the same catalogue, instead of bolting rental on later, kept the experience consistent for both kinds of customer.

Results

  • One catalogue that serves both buyers and renters.
  • Comparison tables for spec-heavy decisions.
