Portrait on Gold

portraitongold.com

Visit Site ↗

The Brief

A custom portrait eCommerce store needed complex product variants (size, frame, finish combinations) without relying on paid Shopify apps, plus a checkout experience that could cut down on fake Cash-on-Delivery orders — a common problem for personalised/made-to-order products in the Indian market.

What I Built

  • App-free JavaScript-based product filtering, replacing what would normally be a paid collection-filter app
  • Custom Liquid logic for complex product variant combinations
  • A WhatsApp-first checkout flow via WATI to verify orders before fulfillment, specifically targeting fake COD reduction
  • Fully mobile-optimised storefront, since the bulk of traffic for personalised gifting comes from mobile ad clicks

Why It Mattered

Personalised/made-to-order products carry real production cost per order, so a fake or unconfirmed COD order is a direct loss. Verifying intent over WhatsApp before fulfillment — rather than after a failed delivery — was the core design decision behind the checkout flow.

Diviniti

diviniti.com

Visit Site ↗

The Brief

A luxury brand store carrying 500+ products needed a full rebuild — the existing image architecture was dragging down load times, the theme had no flexible section system for the merchandising team to use independently, and there was no structured tracking in place to understand customer behaviour.

What I Built

  • Rebuilt the image-loading architecture (responsive sizes, lazy-loading strategy, mobile/desktop image pickers) to cut Largest Contentful Paint by 40%
  • 20+ custom Liquid sections, each schema-driven so the merchandising team can rearrange and edit pages without touching code
  • GTM and Microsoft Clarity integration for heatmaps and structured event tracking
  • Structured on-page SEO across the full 500+ product catalogue

Why It Mattered

For a luxury catalogue this size, load speed directly affects paid-ad efficiency — slow LCP inflates cost-per-click on Google/Meta campaigns. The 40% LCP improvement was the headline metric, but the schema-driven sections were what let the team keep shipping merchandising changes without coming back to me for every edit.

Ratanarpan

ratanarpan.com

Visit Site ↗

The Brief

A premium festive and corporate gifting brand needed a complete online store, plus a steady stream of festival-timed content — without a large team to brief, write, schedule and optimise it.

What I Built

  • The whole Shopify store end to end: theme, homepage sections for collections, festive campaigns, bestsellers, corporate gifts and custom hampers, and every product listing
  • Scheduled tasks (Claude Cowork) that request banners from the design team with a matched in-stock product, send deadline reminders and escalate overdue banners
  • A blog workflow that turns banner emails and a festival calendar into 800–1,200-word SEO drafts with product placements, checked for duplicates
  • Weekly SEO upkeep — meta titles and descriptions refreshed from new keyword research, product images renamed, and missed tasks re-run

Why It Mattered

Automate the routine work — briefs, drafts, scheduling and SEO upkeep — but keep people on the creative and editorial decisions. The design team makes every banner, and every post waits 24 hours for review before it publishes, so a small team ships on time for every festival.

The Majestic India

themajesticindia.com

Visit Site ↗

The Brief

A premium pageant training platform needed a mobile-first WordPress site that could clearly present multiple program/license tiers and funnel visitors toward a single, unambiguous "Apply Now" action — without the page feeling like a generic brochure site.

What I Built

  • Custom WordPress + Elementor build with four distinct license/program pages
  • Mobile-first layout, since most prospective applicants were browsing on phones
  • A consistent, clear Apply Now CTA flow repeated at every key decision point

Why It Mattered

With four separate program tiers, the risk was visitor confusion about which path applied to them. Structuring four dedicated pages instead of one long page made it possible for each visitor to land on content specific to their tier and apply with one clear action.

Uncostly

uncostly.in

Visit Site ↗

The Brief

A security & surveillance equipment retailer needed a WooCommerce catalogue spanning 12+ categories, with the added complexity of supporting both outright purchase and equipment rental — plus comparison tools to help technical buyers evaluate specs side-by-side.

What I Built

  • WooCommerce catalogue architecture across 12+ product categories
  • Side-by-side comparison tables for spec-heavy purchase decisions
  • Wishlist and order-tracking functionality
  • A dedicated Rent section, separate from the standard purchase flow

Why It Mattered

Security equipment buyers are typically comparing specs across multiple SKUs before committing, and a meaningful share of demand is short-term rental rather than purchase. Building both flows into the same catalogue — rather than bolting rental on as an afterthought — kept the buying experience coherent for both types of customer.
