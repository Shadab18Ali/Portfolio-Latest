Who I am

The Short Version

I’m a Full-Stack Developer based in Delhi, India, with 3+ years of web development experience. My work covers Shopify 2.0 storefronts, Liquid and theme architecture, metafields, the Shopify Admin, Storefront and GraphQL APIs, custom Shopify development, headless commerce with React and Next.js, and custom web apps.

Today I’m a Shopify Developer at Kairali Ayurvedic, building theme sections, publishing product data through the Admin GraphQL API and managing production deployments for its .com and .in stores. Before that, as Full Stack Developer for Diviniti and Portrait on Gold, I built the Diviniti and Portrait on Gold Shopify storefronts, and I built Ratanarpan with AI-assisted automation for product and SEO content. I also designed and built FreeToolsHub, a Next.js and TypeScript platform with 74 browser-based tools, and The Daily Ledger, a full-stack newsroom with a public site, a protected admin and scheduled publishing.

Around the storefront I handle backend and API integrations, e-commerce integrations such as WhatsApp order flows, performance optimisation, technical SEO, analytics (GA4, GTM, Meta Pixel, Clarity) and AI automation. WordPress and WooCommerce are a secondary part of my work.
