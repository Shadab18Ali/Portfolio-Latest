Who I am

The Short Version

I’m a Shopify developer based in Delhi, India, with 3+ years of web development experience. Shopify is my main platform — Liquid, Shopify 2.0 theme architecture, metafields and the Admin GraphQL API — with WordPress and WooCommerce alongside it.

Today I’m a Shopify Developer at Kairali Ayurvedic, building theme sections and product data for its .com and .in stores. Before that, at Diviniti, I built and optimised the Diviniti and Portrait on Gold stores, and I built Ratanarpan with an AI-assisted content workflow.

The work goes beyond theme edits: WhatsApp automation through WATI, COD verification to cut fake orders, GTM and Clarity for behaviour tracking, and app-free features that avoid recurring subscription costs.
