04 · How I work

Process

How I Work

  1. 01

    Discover

    We go through the store, the goal and what isn't working today.

  2. 02

    Plan

    A written scope with priorities, timeline and cost before any code.

  3. 03

    Build

    Development in stages, with regular check-ins instead of one big handoff.

  4. 04

    Test

    Cross-device QA, speed checks and tracking verification.

  5. 05

    Launch

    Go live, then check the live store, forms and tracking straight away.

  6. 06

    Optimise

    Measure what changed and keep fixing what the data points to.
